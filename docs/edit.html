Edit parishes & sites

Changes are saved in this browser as you type and immediately used by the map, table and profiles (a yellow banner reminds you). To make them permanent for everyone, download both CSV files, replace data/parishes.csv and data/locations.csv in the repository, and rebuild (see Methods & data). You can also edit the CSVs in Excel and load them here to check them on the map.

Select a parish, then drag its blue pins to correct locations, or click “Place on map” for a site and click the map. Shaded tracts are the parish's nearest-parish area with the current data.
